Privacy Policy

Travel Journal AI  ·  Last updated: 29 August 2026

Travel Journal AI is a personal travel journaling app developed by Gary Crook. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and what is never collected.

What the app accesses

Photo library. The app reads photos from an album you choose. It uses photo image data to generate AI captions and quality scores, and reads GPS coordinates, date, and camera metadata embedded in each photo. This processing happens entirely on your device. No photo or metadata is sent to any external server.

Weather data. The app contacts the Open-Meteo public archive API (open-meteo.com) to fetch historical weather conditions for the locations and dates in your photos. Only approximate GPS coordinates and dates are sent — no personal information. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to these requests.

What the app does NOT collect

The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data outside your device. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no cloud backup of your journals or photos. All AI processing uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework — no data leaves your iPhone or iPad.

On-device AI

Photo captions, quality scoring, and journal text are generated using Apple Intelligence running locally on your device. Apple's own privacy terms govern how the Foundation Models framework operates. Gary Crook receives no output from this processing.

App settings

One preference (whether Apple Intelligence Vision mode is enabled) is stored in your device's local settings (UserDefaults). It is never synced or transmitted.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
