## Description (max 4000 chars)
Travel Journal AI turns your holiday photo albums into beautifully written travel journals — automatically.

Import a photo album from your iPhone's library and the app does the rest. It reads your photos, clusters them by location and day, fetches the weather for each place, generates captions for every image, and writes a magazine-style journal entry for each location you visited — all on your device using Apple Intelligence.

**How it works**
1. Create a trip and pick a photo album
2. The app imports your photos and groups them by location using GPS
3. It fetches real historical weather for each place and day
4. Apple Intelligence captions each photo and scores it for quality
5. A beautifully written journal entry is generated for every location
6. Read, share, or export your finished journal

**Your privacy is protected**
All AI processing runs entirely on your device using Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Your photos and journal entries never leave your iPhone or iPad. No account required. No subscription. No cloud upload.

**Photo management**
- Mark photos as favourites and choose a cover photo for each trip
- Automatically excludes blurry, dark, or duplicate shots
- Override any exclusion decision with a single tap
- Full photo grid view with quality and inclusion status at a glance

**Magazine-quality journals**
Journal entries are written in a concise, first-person style grounded strictly in your photos — the app never invents details it can't see. Entries include the date, location, weather, and a lead photo. Export the full journal as text to share with friends or family.

**Locations**
An interactive map shows every place you visited, with visit cards showing date, weather, and photo count. GPS coordinates are extracted from your photo metadata — no location permission required.

Requires iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence enabled (iPhone 15 Pro or later, or M-series iPad).

## Review Notes (for Apple reviewer)
- The app uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework (FoundationModels) for photo captioning, quality scoring, and journal generation. No data is sent to any external server.
- Photos access is required to import a user-selected album. The app reads photo metadata (GPS, date) and image data for AI captioning.
- Weather data is fetched from the Open-Meteo public archive API (open-meteo.com) — no API key, no personal data sent.
- Apple Intelligence Vision mode (image input to Foundation Models) is off by default and can be toggled in Settings. When off, captions use photo metadata only.
- Test account: not required — all functionality is accessible without login.
