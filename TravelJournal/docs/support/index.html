Common questions

The app says Apple Intelligence is not available.
Apple Intelligence requires iPhone 15 Pro or later (or an M-series iPad) with iOS 18 or later and a supported language. Make sure Apple Intelligence is enabled in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri.

My photos don't have locations on the map.
The app reads GPS from photo metadata. If your camera or phone had location off when photos were taken, they won't have GPS data and won't appear on the map.

The journal generation seems slow.
Journal text is generated on-device by Apple Intelligence. Processing time depends on your device and the number of photos. You can leave the app open in the foreground while it runs.

I want to delete all my journals.
Long-press a trip card on the home screen and choose "Delete Trip". This removes the trip and all its associated photos, locations, and journal entries from the app (your original photos remain in your photo library).
